Step 7 · Admin panel

You run everything from the web

🔒 Cedra · Admin

Dashboard

Rides and revenue today, drivers online, pending approvals and money owed.

Drivers

New applications wait here until you approve them.

Driver details

Check every document, then approve, reject or suspend.

Rides

See every ride and its status. Cancel one if needed.

Pricing

Per vehicle type: a fixed route price, or a price calculated per km.

Route fares

Real street prices between towns, e.g. Minieh → Tripoli: Taxi $5, Tuk-tuk $3.

Billing

Who owes what. Record cash, Whish or OMT payments and activate monthly plans.

Settings

Commission %, monthly fees, LBP rate, max debt and support phone.
